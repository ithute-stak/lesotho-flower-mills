# Phase 70 — Controlled pilot readiness

Decision: NOT READY for an external pilot. Phase 69 CI passed, but a successful automated build does not establish business workflow readiness.

Internal demonstration requires an isolated database, synthetic customer and stock records, validated bundle pricing, and a tested full order-to-delivery flow. Financial email delivery, live payments and actual stock release must stay disabled in demonstrations until separately verified.

External pilot blockers: registered and tested Ithute Auth login and active staff membership, approved tax and prices, independently verified settlement reconciliation, reservation-to-dispatch stock controls, full customer/admin/warehouse/driver browser tests, incident handling, backup/restore drills, and documented business sign-off.

Production launch additionally requires pilot outcomes, security testing, operational monitoring, deployment and rollback procedures, and training.

Next: create a synthetic full-order workflow test plan with a traceable evidence register. No external pilot has been authorised by this audit.
